What changed in this revision

Shown only on the staging preview — hidden in production.

New release notes — first published revision for the 4.18.0 wave.

Scope: CiC 4.18.0-alpha.0 (alpha-tester track, not the general fleet), diffed 4.17.0 → 4.18.0-alpha.0. Paired, customer-live now: App v1.65.0 (2026-11-04) and Cloud v2.51.0 (2026-11-02). Bundled firmware unchanged from 4.17.0: Controller 6.16.1, Thread 2.15.0, HeatCharger 1.3.0, Chill CB 2.0.1, ODU 2.2. 92 ticket rows.

Phase note: headline date 2026-11-05 = the alpha.0 tag commit’s committer date (no GitHub Release published yet). The CiC image is on the alpha-tester track only; the App and Cloud releases are live for customers. The only CiC components that bumped this wave are the C++ apps (embedded-cpp-apps 1.40.3 → 1.41.0), message spec (2.90.0 → 2.94.0) and Python libs (0.18.1 → 0.18.2).

Accuracy calls (component version gating): QPD-16535 ships only the ODU EEPROM-CRC telemetry this wave — the “V1 bottom-plate below 4 °C” fix is still In Review and is described by the shipped commit, not the ticket title. QPD-16661 ships only the wpa_supplicant Axiom diagnostics — the underlying WiFi-association fix is Backlog. Both follow the “describe by the commit that landed in a bumped component” rule.

Release timeline

Live from the shared Quatt release calendar.

    New functions

    • Weather forecast on the Home screen: your Home screen now shows the forecast temperature with a weather icon.
    • Forecast outside temperature in Insights: the day view now draws the forecast outside temperature as a dashed line.
    • Change your referral e-mail: you can now update the e-mail address used for referrals directly in Settings.
    • “Preheating” on the Home screen: when your heat pump is warming the home ahead of your schedule, the Home screen now shows “Preheating” instead of a target temperature.
    • Low central-heating pressure warning: on All-Electric systems, the app now shows a warning when the central-heating water pressure is low.

    Heating performance and reliability

    • Below-zero outside temperature is now handled correctly: freezing outdoor temperatures are passed to a connected ketel and room thermostat as the real negative value instead of 0 °C, so heating stays accurate in cold weather.
    • More reliable communication with OpenTherm ketels and room thermostats.
    • More reliable water-flow measurement, including right after an internal restart, which keeps heating control accurate.
    • Your Chill now reconnects on its own if its wireless link briefly drops, instead of staying offline until the system restarts.

    Bug fixes and maintenance

    • Fixed an issue that could close the app shortly after opening on some Android phones.
    • Fixed a crash that could happen when switching between installations.
    • Removed a false “Can’t connect to the Quatt cloud” message that could briefly appear when reopening the app.
    • Changing the CiC’s Wi-Fi network no longer leaves the app stuck on “connecting”.
    • Fixed the “Forget Wi-Fi” screen not responding to taps on Android.
    • A more stable Wi-Fi connection on the CiC, with fewer unnecessary network scans.
    • Your Chill temperature settings are kept after the CiC restarts.
    • Customers whose phone number is temporarily blocked at login are now guided to a way to continue.
    • Corrected the energy Insights day view on the autumn clock-change day, so the repeated 02:00 hour is no longer dropped.

    Nieuwe functies

    • Weersverwachting op het Home-scherm: je Home-scherm toont nu de verwachte temperatuur met een weer-icoon.
    • Verwachte buitentemperatuur in Insights: de dagweergave tekent de verwachte buitentemperatuur nu als een stippellijn.
    • Je referral-e-mailadres aanpassen: je kunt het e-mailadres voor referrals nu rechtstreeks in Instellingen wijzigen.
    • “Voorverwarmen” op het Home-scherm: als je warmtepomp alvast opwarmt vooruitlopend op je schema, toont het Home-scherm nu “Voorverwarmen” in plaats van een doeltemperatuur.
    • Waarschuwing bij lage cv-druk: bij All-Electric-systemen toont de app nu een waarschuwing als de waterdruk van de centrale verwarming laag is.

    Verwarmingsprestaties en betrouwbaarheid

    • Temperaturen onder nul worden nu juist verwerkt: een vriezende buitentemperatuur wordt aan een aangesloten ketel en kamerthermostaat doorgegeven als de echte negatieve waarde in plaats van 0 °C, zodat het verwarmen ook bij koud weer nauwkeurig blijft.
    • Betrouwbaardere communicatie met OpenTherm-ketels en kamerthermostaten.
    • Betrouwbaardere meting van de waterdoorstroming, ook direct na een interne herstart, waardoor de verwarmingsregeling nauwkeurig blijft.
    • Je Chill maakt nu zelf opnieuw verbinding als de draadloze verbinding even wegvalt, in plaats van offline te blijven tot het systeem herstart.

    Foutoplossingen en onderhoud

    • Een probleem opgelost waardoor de app op sommige Android-telefoons kort na het openen kon sluiten.
    • Een crash opgelost die kon optreden bij het wisselen tussen installaties.
    • Een onterechte melding “Kan geen verbinding maken met de Quatt-cloud” verwijderd die kort kon verschijnen bij het heropenen van de app.
    • Het wijzigen van het Wi-Fi-netwerk van de CiC laat de app niet langer hangen op “verbinden”.
    • Het scherm “Wi-Fi vergeten” reageert nu weer op tikken op Android.
    • Een stabielere Wi-Fi-verbinding op de CiC, met minder onnodige netwerkscans.
    • Je temperatuurinstellingen voor de Chill blijven behouden na een herstart van de CiC.
    • Klanten van wie het telefoonnummer tijdelijk geblokkeerd is bij het inloggen, worden nu naar een manier geleid om door te gaan.
    • De dagweergave van energie-Insights op de dag van de herfstklokwisseling is gecorrigeerd, zodat het herhaalde uur van 02:00 niet meer wegvalt.

    QPD-16025 · EnergyOS → cloud-backend building config sync 17 tickets

    A new backend pipeline that mirrors EnergyOS building configuration into the Quatt cloud: a worker and message handler, a BuildingConfig mapper and Avro decoder, link resolution with stale-link and serial guards, a review queue with a CLI, drift reporting, metrics and alerts. Inverter-replacement detection and application sit behind a mode flag.

    • Resolution and review: link resolution with guards (phase 1) (QPD-16032 Cloud / Backend); commissioning must not overwrite sync-owned values (QPD-14804 Cloud / Backend); phase-gated apply of identity and config columns (QPD-16034 Cloud / Backend); review queue with dedupe and auto-close (QPD-16314 Cloud / Backend); mirror diff and pendingDiff (QPD-16315 Cloud / Backend); resolve new buildings and tombstones (QPD-16316 no team set); review CLI (QPD-16321 no team set)

    • Observability: metrics (QPD-16035 Cloud / Backend), drift report (QPD-16036 Cloud / Backend), Grafana alerts (QPD-16320 no team set)
    Impact for ops: none yet — this ships dark and applies inverter replacements only behind a mode flag. It is groundwork for keeping HomeBattery/EnergyOS building data in sync; do not present it as a live capability.

    QPD-11880 · Bug Fixing & Fleet Diagnostics 13 tickets

    The embedded bug-fixing and fleet-diagnostics Epic. This wave lands the CiC firmware fixes (C++ apps 1.41.0, message spec 2.94.0) heading to the alpha track: OpenTherm boiler/thermostat correctness, water-flow measurement, Chill connection reliability, Wi-Fi tuning and remote-support/observability work.

    • OpenTherm ketel and thermostat correctness: decode/encode OpenTherm f8.8 values as signed, so a below-zero outside temperature is no longer sent as 0 °C (QPD-16539 Embedded Systems); publish only acknowledged boiler data and stop polling unsupported IDs (QPD-16475 Embedded Systems); validate thermostat message types and reply per spec (QPD-16476 Embedded Systems); investigate and harden the OpenTherm DHW path, publish the boiler member ID and product type (QPD-16334 Embedded Systems)
    • Water-flow measurement: the flowmeter stopped counting after a cic-flow restart until a reboot (QPD-16407 Embedded Systems); cic-flow cut the measured pulse period to whole seconds (QPD-16379 Embedded Systems)
    • Chill connection reliability: cic-chill kept a dead CoAP session after its observes failed, leaving the Chill disconnected until a restart (QPD-16562 Embedded Systems); cancel_observe use-after-free hardened (QPD-16257 no team set); app hung on “connecting” after a Wi-Fi switch because cic-cloud-proxy dropped the reply (QPD-16631 Embedded Systems)
    • Connectivity and diagnostics: Wi-Fi roaming tuning, prefer 2.4 GHz (QPD-16169 Embedded Systems); ship wpa_supplicant connection events to Axiom — diagnostics for a saved-network association failure whose root fix is still open (QPD-16661 Embedded Systems); ship the full kernel OOM report and bound command runtime (QPD-16683 Embedded Systems); keep the remote terminal alive on LTE-only CiCs by refreshing the Mender token (QPD-16596 Embedded Systems); publish the EEPROM CRC of every ODU (telemetry) (QPD-16535 Embedded Systems)
    On the alpha track only: these CiC firmware fixes are not on the general fleet this wave. Several are customer-perceivable once they roll out (see the Customer tab), but do not promise them to customers or installers until the firmware ships beyond alpha.
    Impact for CS: once rolled out, expect fewer OpenTherm boiler/thermostat quirks (including correct freezing outdoor temperatures), steadier water-flow measurement and self-healing Chill connections. QPD-16661 and QPD-16535 are diagnostics/telemetry only this wave — the bottom-plate-below-4 °C fix and the Wi-Fi-association fix behind those two keys are not yet shipped.
