1 · Chill cooling — condensation quick fix CHILL | Field Test Stability Improvement

  • Raise Chill cooling supply-temperature bounds 18–25°C → 20–27°C (band width kept identical to avoid short-cycling / compressor undershoot, notably on V1 / V1.5 units), and additionally gate cooling on the ODU outlet temperature (QPD-14755, Urgent)
Impact for CS: during the recent heat wave several customers (incl. public reports on Tweakers) saw condensation form on the cooling water pipes — the indoor dew point rose above the supply-water temperature. This deliberate quick fix shifts both cooling bounds up by 2°C to keep the supply water above the dew point under extreme indoor humidity. A permanent solution is tracked separately. Expect condensation complaints on cooling Chills to drop on 4.8.1.

2 · Commissioning Bug Fixing & Fleet Diagnostics

  • V1 commissioning heat-pump test frequency lowered 49 Hz → 30 Hz (QPD-14749)
Impact for installers: the V1 heat-pump commissioning test now runs the compressor at 30 Hz instead of 49 Hz. Installer-only change — not customer-visible.
