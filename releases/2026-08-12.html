    QPD-11879 · 26.Q1 Embedded Technical Debt 1 ticket

    The Q1-2026 embedded technical-debt Epic. In the 4.11.0 wave, QPD-15294 taught the HeatCharger Modbus driver to stop polling (park itself) on systems the configuration reports as having no heat charger, to save CPU on Hybrid/DUO systems. This hotfix reverts that behaviour after it misfired in production.

    • Revert “park the HeatCharger Modbus driver when the system reports no heat charger”: some production All-Electric systems were configured as having no HeatCharger (hasHeatcharger: false) even though one was present, so the CiC stopped polling it and All-Electric data stopped being collected. The parking condition is removed, so the driver always polls the HeatCharger again (QPD-15294 Embedded Systems)
    Impact for CS / ops: All-Electric installations that were incorrectly configured as having no HeatCharger had stopped reporting HeatCharger and All-Electric data to the cloud — visible as gaps in HeatBattery and energy data in the app and dashboards. After they receive 4.11.1 they collect and report this data normally again. If a customer or CS reported missing HeatBattery/energy data on an All-Electric system on 4.11.0, this hotfix resolves it. The backend team is separately investigating the configuration that caused the mismatch; the small CPU saving on genuine no-HeatCharger systems is given up in the meantime.
