Heating performance and reliability

  • Fixed an issue where cooling with your Chill could stop working on some systems: the outdoor unit did not switch to cooling and the water warmed up instead of cooling down. Cooling now runs correctly again on the affected systems.

Bug fixes and maintenance

  • This update also includes general reliability improvements.

1 · Chill cooling — regression fix Bug Fixing & Fleet Diagnostics

  • The outdoor unit now switches to cooling based on actual heating demand, not on the raw central-heating-enable signal from the thermostat (QPD-15262, High)
Impact for CS / ops: on installations with a thermostat that keeps central-heating enable switched on all year (for example a Remeha Celcia 20), a cooling Chill could run while the outdoor unit stayed idle — so instead of cooling, the water gradually warmed up. This was a regression introduced in the 4.8.0 release. Around 6 Chill installations were affected (tracked in NCR-3862); they will resume cooling normally once they receive 4.9.1. If a customer reported "the Chill is on but not cooling" on 4.8.0–4.9.0, this hotfix resolves it.
